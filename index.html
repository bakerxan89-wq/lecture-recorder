⚠ About live transcription: The browser's Web Speech API listens to the microphone — it does not automatically transcribe arbitrary captured tab audio. When you record a browser tab for audio, you will hear the class in the recording, but live captions will come from your microphone (what the browser's recognition hears). For accurate tab-audio transcription a backend speech-to-text API (e.g. Whisper, Google STT) would be required. Audio recording always works and is independent of transcription.
